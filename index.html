Gesture laboratory

Your hands are the controllers.

MediaPipe reads 21 landmarks per hand at a locked 30 frames per second. Smoothing, dead zones, and fist hysteresis keep every motion stable.

GESTURE ROTATE
  1. G1

    Open hand rotate

    Move one open hand to orbit the model. Rotation eases toward your palm, never snaps.

  2. G2

    Fist zoom

    Close a fist and push toward or pull away from the camera to zoom the field.

  3. G3

    Two hands scale

    Spread both hands apart to enlarge the model, bring them together to shrink it.

  4. G4

    Pinch grab

    Touch thumb and index finger with Sculpt Field on to grab and move nearby particles.
