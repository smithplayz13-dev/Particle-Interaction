SYSTEM GUIDE

LUMINA // SPATIAL PARTICLE ENGINE

One hand — rotate. Move your open hand to steer the particle field.

Fist — zoom. Make a fist, push closer or pull away to scale the field.

Two hands — scale. Spread apart to enlarge, pinch together to shrink.

Pinch — grab. Touch thumb and index to sculpt particles. Modes: attract, repel, drag.

Mouse — orbit. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan.

Sculpt field. Enable grab, pick a mode, tune brush radius and field strength.
